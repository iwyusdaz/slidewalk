# Educator review template — slidewalk reference deck

**Status: protocol ready, review pending.** Fill this in with a real educator or instructional designer. Until then, `evaluation/METHODS.md` keeps the results table empty.

## For the reviewer (educator)

You are reviewing a 17-slide lesson deck, "Intro to WCAG for content authors", and its facilitator guide. The deck was generated by a tool (slidewalk) from a lesson outline. Please spend 20–30 minutes.

Materials: `docs/deck.html` (open in a browser; arrows to navigate, N for speaker notes), `docs/deck.pptx` (optional), `docs/facilitator-guide.md`, `examples/wcag-authors/outline.md` (the source outline).

## Review form

**Reviewer role** (instructional designer / teacher / curriculum developer / other): ____________
**Date:** ____________
**Years of experience:** ____________

### A. Content quality
1. Is any statement in the deck technically wrong or misleading about WCAG? (slide numbers, please)
2. Is anything important missing for a first exposure at this level?
3. Are the objectives ("Learners can ...") observable and teachable? Which are weakest?

### B. The auto-generated checkpoints
4. The checkpoint questions are template-generated from slide objectives and are labeled as such on the slides. Read them as a learner would: are any of them confusing, trivial, or unanswerable from the preceding slides?
5. Would you replace any prompt with your own? Which?

### C. Facilitator guide
6. Could you teach from the facilitator guide without having written the deck? What's missing?
7. Is the guidance about pausing at checkpoints clear enough to actually change behavior?

### D. Usability
8. Time yourself flipping through the HTML deck by keyboard. Any friction?
9. If you received the PPTX from a colleague, would it look "normal" in PowerPoint? Anything odd?

### E. Overall
10. Would you use (a lightly edited version of) this deck with your own audience? Why / why not?
11. The tool claims only "gated against automated WCAG 2.1 A/AA checks". Does that framing feel honest, overcautious, or overclaiming to you?
